# Audit of the room, version 3, and what followed it, October 2026

Written 2026-10-23 by OpenCode 3 on the branch `feat/q3-read-what-the`, from `c699442` to `2ab6864` (0.1.177 to 0.1.201), for brief Q3. It reads what went into `src/`, `server/`, `scripts/` and `bin/` in that span: 47 commits, 37 files, 3609 lines added and 1239 removed (`git diff --stat c699442 refs/heads/main -- src server scripts bin`). The last commit only adds briefs; the code is the 0.1.200 merge. This is a report only. No code changed; the only other file this brief writes is `docs/agents/CHANGELOG.md`. File and line numbers are those of `2ab6864`.

The audits before this one are `AUDIT-2026-10.md` (the bridge and the pure logic), `AUDIT-2026-10-UI.md` (the browser) and `AUDIT-2026-10-DELTA.md` (0.1.121 to 0.1.152). This review does not repeat their findings. It looks at the room version 3: the sphere layout, the parked agents and their strip, subagents, follow mode, the settings panel and the screen effects, the cost board as a screen panel, and the depth plane.

## Severity

- High: a normal load or a normal click shows the room wrong.
- Medium: a normal case, such as a seek, a failed tool call or a folder change, shows wrong output, or a resource grows over a session, with no lasting harm.
- Low: odd input, extra work per frame, or slow growth with no lasting harm.

## What was checked and found sound

- The room's dispose paths. `room.dispose` cancels the frame, clears the 250 ms timers, disconnects the resize observer, removes the two WebGL context listeners, disposes the camera rig, agents, terminals, board, labels, strings, cards, hub, space and renderer, removes the canvas and the CSS3D layer, and disposes the shared glow texture. `camera.dispose` removes the five pointer and wheel listeners from the surface and window. `useBridgeHealth`, `Stage`, `Hud` and the panels remove their intervals and key listeners on cleanup. The agent dispose test disposes every geometry, material and texture and empties the scene; `removeView` disposes the shape, rings, label map, picker, links, strip and callout, and `cards.removeCard` disposes the occluder geometry and removes the element. The exceptions are V1, V6 and V8.
- The seek pipeline. `player.seek` discards the state and replays every activity up to the target, then calls `onReset`; `room.sync` clears labels and pulses, `cards.sync` clears flashes, and `agents.snap` clears aim, pointer, strip opacity, group position, style, fade time and done time. The exceptions are V2, V4 and V15.
- Finite values and guards. `sizeOf`, `normalizeObstacles` and `previousAt` reject non-finite sphere inputs; `fitView` starts at `FIT_MIN` and caps at `MAX_DIST`; `camera.update` resets when the goal is not finite; `cards.screenFrame` returns null when a projection is not finite; `settings.js` clamps every effect strength and the depth; the board clamps its bar width and formats non-finite money and tokens. I found no path from the parsers, state, plan or settings to a NaN or Infinity position or scale in the room.
- The sphere layout and the overview. `layoutSphere` keeps the root at the hub centre below it, puts top-level folders inside the pole caps, keeps pairs and obstacles apart, and moves only the new, resized or obstructed cards and their siblings on a warm start. `overviewSpheres` filters a card without a finite home and takes the root and the cards with `pinned: true`; the room's refit keeps the 150 unit and 8 percent thresholds and never goes below 1800. The probe's `sphereChain` and `replayDigest` use the same functions.
- The strings and agents. Straight lines, seeded strands, the 1.5 second yellow fade, the 0.25 opacity dimming and the 1 second return are in the code; `strandPoints` is pure and tested even though the room's fill path uses the cached offsets. Agents stay parked, the orchestrator is at `HUB_Y + ORCHESTRATOR_LIFT`, the strip has three segments, the four brackets are in `cards.js` and shown through `.is-targeted`, and `followTarget` applies the 4 second hold, the middle two thirds and the 1.2 second ease. The failed-call case is V5.
- The subagents. `state.js` adds a child with parent and depth up to 3, the parent's `run.end` ends its working children, `childPlace` keeps the parent's height and radius, and `childFade` holds for 6 seconds and fades for 1.5 (at once after the hold under reduced motion). The child's terminal title, status ring and HUD row are built. The exceptions are V8 and V9.
- The settings and the screen effects. `effectSettings` fills defaults, clamps strengths and depth, and persists in `machineroom.settings`. The vignette and grain are a CSS overlay, dust is a points field, and fish eye and aberration share one SVG filter on the layer element; the board is deliberately outside that filter and above the overlay, as `ARCHITECTURE.md:134` now says. The filter turns each effect off at zero strength. I did not judge the look.
- The cost board. It is a fixed 280 px panel, hidden below 900 px, with a row per reported agent, a clamped share bar, quota lines and the empty message; a row click calls `onPick`; the camera skips `.mr-board` on pointer and wheel; it takes no part in the fit or layout. The V19 review's note about the source of its panel is correct.
- The bridge's tree path and the scale tools. `startTreeRead` keeps one read in flight, `treeDirty` starts exactly one more, `GET /api/tree` reuses a folder answer from the last second, the watcher is closed on the first change, the poll waits three times the last read and stops after two quiet seconds, and `stop` clears the poll timer, the watcher, the surface timer, the reconnect timer, the socket and the clients. `scale-live` starts and stops its bridge child, closes its stream and removes the checkout unless `--keep`; `scale-probe` resets the folder index between samples. V14 is the exception on the watcher re-arm.
- The reduced motion paths. `still()` follows the media query through `mql.matches` and is read by the camera, cards, labels, hub, void, strings, agents and room; the card fade, camera transition, dim, strip, callout, strand, label, spin, dust and dash ring each have the documented still branch. I did not check the look.
- The numbers in `ARCHITECTURE.md` I compared with the code: the hub radius and lift, the shell radii, the 0.4 second card fade, the 1.2 second follow ease, the 4 second hold, the 0.25 dim, the 1 second return, the 1800 minimum fit, the 280 px board, the 900 px threshold, the 60 second health read, the 6 second child hold, the 1.5 second child fade and the 24 unit strand spacing all match. The only mismatch I found is V16.
