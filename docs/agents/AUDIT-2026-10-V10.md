# Audit of the page's handlers, version 10

Written 2026-10-30 by OpenCode 2 on the branch `feat/a10-audit-the-page`, at `dd9c236` (0.1.401), for brief A10. It reads the 25 `src/ui/*.jsx` files and the 8 `src/ui/panels/*.jsx` files, 11,966 lines together, and the helpers and client they call. File and line numbers are those of commit `dd9c236`. The test suite at that commit has 3391 tests in 192 files. The branch was cut from `refs/heads/main` at `dd9c236`.

The audit before this one is `AUDIT-2026-10-V9.md` (the Direct API keys and the orchestrator wake rules). This audit reads every event handler and effect in those 33 files for what a render test cannot see: a handler that throws before its `try`, a busy flag with no `finally`, a missing prop or optional method called without a check, a stale closure over `selected` or another value, a promise with no `catch`, and an effect with no cleanup. It also reads `orchestratorChat.js`, `family.js`, `modelRefresh.js`, `sidebar.js`, `launcher.js`, `sound.js`, `panels/runs.js`, `panels/reviews.js` and `panels/notices.js`, `src/control/client.js` for the method shapes, and `server/orchestrators.js` for what a ladder write replaces. It does not repeat the findings of earlier audits.

The brief says the tests render on the server and never run a click. That is no longer fully true. `tests/family-tree-ui.test.js` mounts FamilyTree into a hand-made DOM and clicks it 21 times, and `tests/mcp-settings.test.js`, `tests/setup.test.js`, `tests/guide.test.js`, `tests/waiting.test.js` and `tests/notice-empty.test.js` do the same for their screens. None of them clicks the screens or the sequences below, and the one test of the family tree's chat button checks only that the event is dispatched, not that a chat opens (`tests/family-tree-ui.test.js:726-744`). The hand-made DOM has no layout, no media queries and no pointer events, so it could not see Z1, Z2, Z6 or Z12 even with a click test. No test at all runs Z3, Z4, Z5, Z7 to Z11.

## Severity

- High: lasting harm, a key leak, or an action on the machine the owner did not confirm.
- Medium: wrong execution or state, a lost update, or runs that keep starting and spend funds.
- Low: an edge case, an inconsistency, an ugly message, or a fallback with no lasting harm.

No finding is High. Nothing here reads or prints a key, runs a command the owner did not confirm, or writes outside the project. Z1, Z2, Z3 and Z4 are Medium: Z1 leaves the main way to open a sub conversation dead on a narrow window, Z2 can continue a different conversation from the one the owner clicked, Z3 keeps starting a confirmed queue after the panel that showed it is gone, and Z4 can drop a sub orchestrator that was added while a confirm card was open. Z5 to Z12 are Low.

## What was checked and found sound

- Busy flags clear on every path in the confirm cards and the forms that have a `finally`: `FamilyTree.runConfirm` (`src/ui/FamilyTree.jsx:507-522`) and `toggleLane` (`:894-916`), `Sidebar.runConfirm` (`src/ui/Sidebar.jsx:102-116`), `setFlag` (`:195-215`) and `saveRename` (`:223-254`), `FamilyTeam.runConfirm` (`src/ui/FamilyTeam.jsx:181-195`), `ReviewsPanel.handleConfirmMerge` (`src/ui/panels/ReviewsPanel.jsx:213-237`), `CompactCard.runCompact` (`src/ui/AgentStrip.jsx:23-46`), `AutocompactInput.save` (`src/ui/AutocompactInput.jsx:74-95`), `SetupCard.start` (`src/ui/SetupCard.jsx:82-102`), `EndpointsPanel.save`, `remove`, `refreshModels` and `fetchModels` (`src/ui/EndpointsPanel.jsx:243-261`, `:317-334`, `:336-355`, `:357-380`), `OrchestratorChat.saveModel` and `saveEffort` (`src/ui/OrchestratorChat.jsx:573-628`), and the MCP and search saves. The split project run in Z3 is the one path without a `finally` or a cancel.
- The Escape key on a confirm card does not close the page over it. FamilyTree (`src/ui/FamilyTree.jsx:307-317`), Sidebar (`src/ui/Sidebar.jsx:73-83`) and FamilyTeam (`src/ui/FamilyTeam.jsx:99-109`) listen in the capture phase and call `stopPropagation`, so Hud's bubble listener at `src/ui/Hud.jsx:503-538` never sees that key. ControlDrawer's own Escape (`src/ui/panels/ControlDrawer.jsx:83-93`) closes the drawer, which is the panel that owns it.
- Effects clean up after themselves. Hud removes its resize listener, clears its two 60 second timers, unsubscribes its stream listener and removes both key listeners (`src/ui/Hud.jsx:309-315`, `:386-408`, `:410-445`, `:477-489`, `:503-538`). Guide removes its resize and scroll listeners, its interval and its MutationObserver (`src/ui/Guide.jsx:122-149`). Launcher removes `machineroom:projects` (`src/ui/Launcher.jsx:60-80`). ControlDrawer removes its key listener (`src/ui/panels/ControlDrawer.jsx:83-93`). LensRail removes its wheel listener (`src/ui/LensRail.jsx:10-20`). MusicPlayer removes its ended listener on every render (`src/ui/MusicPlayer.jsx:91-104`). Assistant removes its key listener and its resize listener (`src/ui/Assistant.jsx:74-148`), but not the drag listeners in Z6.
- The reads that need an order guard already have one in two places: DispatchPanel's prompt (`promptReqRef`, `src/ui/panels/DispatchPanel.jsx:205-226`) and ReviewsPanel's diff (`diffReqRef`, `src/ui/panels/ReviewsPanel.jsx:143-160`). The rest of the panel reads use a `mounted` or `active` flag or none; only Z2 and Z10 show a wrong result from the missing ticket.
- Effects that can outlive their component use an `active` or `stopped` flag: FamilyTree (`src/ui/FamilyTree.jsx:176-211`, `:260-305`, `:469-498`), FamilyTeam (`src/ui/FamilyTeam.jsx:36-68`), EndpointsPanel (`src/ui/EndpointsPanel.jsx:163-189`), McpSettings (`src/ui/McpSettings.jsx:233-253`), SearchSettings (`src/ui/SearchSettings.jsx:107-132`), Launcher (`src/ui/Launcher.jsx:102-149`), SetupCard (`src/ui/SetupCard.jsx:37-64`) and OrchestratorChat (`src/ui/OrchestratorChat.jsx:233-310`, `:312-358`). The panels' plain reads (Tasks, Meters, issue comments, the reviews list) have no flag; a late `setState` on them only warns, except the ticket cases in Z10.
- Optional client methods are checked before a write in FamilyTree, Sidebar, FamilyTeam, OrchestratorChat, EndpointsPanel, McpSettings, SearchSettings and DispatchPanel. The exceptions are the plain reads in Z11. The client shapes the handlers use match `src/control/client.js`: `setLadder(ladder)` posts `{ ladder, by: 'owner', confirmed: true }` (`:378`), `setAutocompact` accepts both the object and the three argument forms (`:233-270`), `setActiveLanes` accepts `{ lanes, confirmed }` (`:437-451`), and `briefPrompt`, `startRun`, `mergeReview`, `saveMcpServer`, `deleteMcpServer`, `callMcpTool`, `saveEndpoint`, `quickEndpoint` and `endpointModels` take the arguments the panels pass.

## What this session could not check

- A browser or a screen. The click, drag and pointercancel sequences in Z1, Z2, Z6 and Z12, the stacking and z-index of the floating chat over the sessions overlay, the 900 pixel layout switch, focus restoration and the saved terminal position are left to the orchestrator.
- A live bridge, an orchestrator or a lane run. Z3 needs a real project queue of three or more tasks and a panel close in the middle; Z4 needs a real ladder change while a confirm card is open; Z9 needs a failing sub read. No bridge, lane run or agent run was started.
- A client that throws synchronously. Z8 is a code read of a pattern that the real async client cannot reach; it matters for stubs and older adapters, and no such client was mounted.
- The actual `localStorage` and saved positions. Z6's reopen position and Z12's replay layout were read, not run.
- The full ladder and session flows against the real server. Only `ladderChange`'s replace behavior was read, at `server/orchestrators.js:499-519`.
- No dev server was started for an eye check, by the house rule for this run.
