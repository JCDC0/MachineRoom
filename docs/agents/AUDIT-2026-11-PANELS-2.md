## Severity

- High: a secret reaches the page, or a feature fails in normal use.
- Medium: the page can send, save or show the wrong thing, or lose what the owner just did, without a way to tell.
- Low: an edge case, a keyboard or screen reader gap, a stale label, or an error the page hides.
