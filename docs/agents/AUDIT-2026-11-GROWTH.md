# Audit 2026-11: Unbounded Growth in a Day-Long Live Room

Scope: Bridge (`server/`, `scripts/run-lane.mjs`) and page (`src/`) during an 8-hour live session with 6 active lanes (~120 lane runs at 15 to 20 minutes per run, ~180 orchestrator and sub-orchestrator turns, ~200,000 SSE frames, ~80,000 tool calls, and ~900 SSE `change` notices). Excluded per brief because they are already being fixed elsewhere: `server/claude-sessions.js` evicted sessions and `server/opencode-sessions.js` finished sessions.
