**S46. Keep hidden lanes out of the active list, sub prompts and ladder edits, and guard empty team applies.**
Six small fixes to finish `S45` and `U162`:
1. In `server/active-lanes.js:353`, filter `eligible` by `!hidden.has(lane.key)` when building `total` and `lanes` in `list()`, so hidden lanes only appear in `hiddenLanes` and never in `LanesSettings`'s `Active lanes` list or `teamLanePool`.
2. In `server/lanes-source.js:244,309` and `server/orchestrator.js:1864`, filter out lanes hidden in the current project (`projectLanesFileFor(roomRoot, folder)`) so `/api/control/agents`, the cost board, and orchestrator run wakeups ignore hidden lanes.
3. In `server/orchestrators.js:334-335`, filter hidden lanes out of `current.lanes` and `ladder.subs` before building `subPrompt`, and in `orchestrators.setLadder` (`server/orchestrators.js:383`) preserve any saved lanes on an existing sub that are currently hidden in this project so UI ladder edits in `FamilyTree` and `FamilyTeam` do not strip them or fail when all of a sub's lanes are hidden.
4. In `server/projects.js:266` and `src/ui/setup.js:102`, report `hasActiveLanes` on each recent project and offer `Copy from PROJECT` on `SetupCard` only when `hasActiveLanes === true`; in `server/teams.js:240,381`, return `409` when `appliedLanes.length === 0` instead of writing `active-lanes.json` with `[]` or hiding all eligible lanes.
5. In `src/ui/FamilyTree.jsx:186,304,347` and `server/teams.js:206,306`, surface `answer.ladderError` in `FamilyTeams` status text, emit `notify('family')` when a team is saved or deleted, and refresh `FamilyTeams` on `family` notices.
6. In `server/teams.js:214,301` and `server/family.js:458,579,685`, catch `URIError` from `decodeURIComponent` and return `400`.
