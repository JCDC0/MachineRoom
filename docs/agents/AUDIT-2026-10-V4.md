# Audit of the room, version 4, and what followed version 3, October 2026

Written 2026-10-24 by OpenCode on the branch `feat/q4-audit-what-changed`, from `b1cff93` to `24a6704` (0.1.201 to 0.1.222), for brief Q4. That was `refs/heads/main` when this branch was made; `refs/heads/main` moved to `621547b` (0.1.227) while the audit ran, with the S4 and F7 lanes, their docs and a bridge poll fix. This audit does not read them, since the brief runs S4 and F7 beside Q4. It reads what went into `src/`, `server/`, `scripts/` and `bin/` in the span: 42 commits, 15 files, 889 lines added and 635 removed (`git diff --stat b1cff93 24a6704 -- src server scripts bin`). The commits after the 0.1.218 merge add tests, briefs and docs; the code is the 0.1.218 merge. This was a report only when it was written. Brief Q4a fixed W1, W4 and W5 in 0.1.229; the Fix column says what changed. File and line numbers are those of `24a6704`.

The audits before this one are `AUDIT-2026-10.md` (the bridge and the pure logic), `AUDIT-2026-10-UI.md` (the browser) and `AUDIT-2026-10-DELTA.md` (0.1.121 to 0.1.152). This review does not repeat their findings. It looks at the briefs version 3 left open, Q3a to Q3d (merged at 0.1.212, 0.1.213, 0.1.215 and 0.1.216), and the two that followed, V21 (the cabinet sphere, 0.1.218) and V22 (packets and travelling strands, 0.1.217), plus the small changes to the HUD keys, the bridge watcher, the fake Paperclip and the scale probe. It reads the open and stack rules, the warm start, the strip and its packets, the strands, follow mode, the seek and reduced motion paths, and the dispose paths.

## Severity

- High: a normal load or a normal click shows the room wrong.
- Medium: a normal case, such as a seek, a failed tool call or a folder change, shows wrong output, or a resource grows over a session, with no lasting harm.
- Low: odd input, extra work per frame, or slow growth with no lasting harm.

## What was checked and found sound

- Finite values and guards. A Node fuzz of 400 random trees (paths up to depth 4, a width of 0, a negative height, NaN and a string among the sizes, random open sets that include `''` and paths not in the plan, and a random previous map with positions up to 5000 units) found no non-finite position, width or height and nothing above 1e6 units. `unit` falls back to `(0, 1, 0)` at zero length, `previousAt` rejects a non-finite previous position, `sizeOf` replaces a bad size, `normalizeObstacles` drops a bad obstacle, and `topShellRadius` clamps to 1100 and 2600. A card with no parent in the plan becomes a top card on the shell (`parentPathOf`), and the root on an open path changes nothing because the root is never a parent in the walk. The e2e room test also checks every placed point of a full fake-Paperclip replay.
- The stack and `stackOverflow`. 300 random plans, run with the rule the room uses (`drawn` without the hidden views, then `layoutSphere`): every drawn view was placed, every hidden view was not, and every placed view was drawn. `stackOverflow`'s parent walk matches `parentPathOf`, its sort matches the `children` order, and both hide the same children. `back` is capped at 3 and counts the hidden children once; a parent that is itself hidden loses its own overflow count, which is shown nowhere, and that is a display limit, not a wrong count. W1 is the plan against both, not the two against each other.
- The warm start. The same entries and open set give the same places cold and warm, and a warm start with no change moved nothing (worst 0). The top search only places a new top; a kept top keeps its exact home; `separateWarmTops` uses `min(width)`, so growing one card does not move its neighbours. At 14, 40, 80 and 120 tops I could not make it move an unchanged card at all, because the Fibonacci shell is already wider than 0.6 of a card at those counts. At 1000 top cards it moves many, and at 3000 and 4000 it moves them by up to 5100 units without settling, while the stacks keep their old places (0 of the moved cards' children followed). The plan draws at most about 300 always-present cards (`maxAlways` 300) plus a few focus folders, so that case is not reachable from the folder plan, but the rule that a stack follows its parent is not in the code. `wasFan` is safe at the sizes the room draws: a fan is 650 units from its parent, a stack is at most about 215, and the threshold is 325.
- Seek. `player.seek` calls `onReset`; `room.sync` clears the labels, the pulses and the `createLater` timers, `cards.sync` clears the flashes and each row's cached file and counts, `agentSet.snap` clears the aim, the pointer, the strip opacity, the home position and the packet send list, and the follow target is re-aimed from the newest touch (W5 is the open focus). The existing `tests/cards-frames.test.js` keeps the same row element after a write and a seek to a state with a read only, and its class and note change to read; the V2 fix holds.
- Reduced motion. `packetSpots` returns nothing and `agents.update` calls `hidePackets` on every frame when `still()` is true; the strands keep their yellow level with no travel; the card fade, list fade, glide, grow, dim, camera transition, label rise, dash ring rotation, dust and motes all jump or stop. The strand yellow after a seek is the documented 1.5 second fade, not a leftover, because the points stop at once; when the seek changes the link set, `relink` rebuilds the strands and the yellow is gone at once. I did not check the look.
- Dispose. The new pieces are all released: `room.dispose` removes the capture click listener on the CSS3D element, clears `createLater`, calls `disposeStill`, and `agentSet.removeView` queues a chip removal that `terminals.remove` takes (V8), while every chip takes a click (V9). The Q3d texture is one per `createStrings` and freed once (V6), and `setEnds` skips an unchanged write (V11). `camera.dispose` removes the five pointer and wheel listeners, the resize observer is disconnected, and the frame is cancelled.
- The bridge and the scripts. `armWatcher` returns false when `watch` throws, `startWatcher` and the poll fall back to `beginPolling(false)`, and the poll keeps reading on its time gate while the watcher cannot be armed; `stop` closes the watcher and clears the poll, the surface and the reconnect timers. The fake Paperclip's new Orchestrator and its fixture keys are a test tool; the scale probe's `topCovered` uses the same `viewBasis` and `fitView` as the room. No finding.
- The numbers. OPEN_STEP 650, STACK_STEP 50, STACK_LIFT 12, STACK_SIDE 16, STACK_LIMIT 4, the 3 back layers, TOP_SPACING 0.6, the 1100 to 2600 shell, the 0.45 second packet and 90 ms interval, the 5 packet burst 30 ms apart, the 24 packet pool, 2000 units a second and the 1.5 second strand fade all match `ARCHITECTURE.md`. Two lines in that file pull against each other: `ARCHITECTURE.md:10` says every folder down to depth 2 is always there, `ARCHITECTURE.md:127` says at most 4 children off the path are drawn. W1 is where they meet; the file is the owner's to settle.

## What this session could not check

- Anything that needs a screen or a browser. No dev server was started. W1's look, W2's green packets, W3's cost, W5's muted chips and W6's keys are left to the orchestrator.
- A real Paperclip. It was not started, no route was called and no run was made; the engine probes used `createState`, `apply`, the folder plan and the layout directly. The brief allowed starting the real Paperclip to check route shapes; it was not needed.
- The memory and GPU cost of W3 and W4. No heap or GPU measurement was taken.
- The full scale probes. I did not run `npm run scale-probe` or `npm run scale-live`; the plan probes used small trees, and the layout probe used random trees and up to 4000 top cards for the warm start.
- The browser's focus behaviour behind a dialog (W6) and the Stage and room cooperation (W5). Both are code findings.
- A card plan at 200,000 files. The fuzz covered the layout at up to 4000 nodes, not the 300 card plan at that tree size.
